NCCB Commission Compensation & Performance Platform v2.0

Policy revision incorporated from Mr. Dickson's revised commission schedule plus Lawrence Hill's confirmed performance logic.

Key policy logic:
- NCCB employees: 3 qualifying sales per workday (aggregate across qualifying services).
- NCCB affiliates: 5 qualifying sales per week to maintain active affiliate status.
- DMP qualifying debt minimum: $10,000.
- DSP qualifying debt minimum: $5,000.
- Employee and affiliate commission formulas are separate.
- Commission eligibility remains tied to payment received/collected/cleared.
- Employee commissions: bi-weekly with NCCB payroll.
- Affiliate commissions: bi-weekly NCCB commission cycle.
- Reversal/recovery language included for canceled/refunded/reversed transactions.
