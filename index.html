Development Planning

Use this workspace to test accelerators, retention incentives, quota alternatives and chargeback-policy revisions before management approval. Proposed changes do not alter the official commission schedule until approved.

Official Current Plan

Commissions are earned on fully funded, collected and cleared revenue and paid bi-weekly with NCCB payroll. The source schedule states no draw or advance is available and requires repayment of commission when a client cancels, terminates or receives a refund.
